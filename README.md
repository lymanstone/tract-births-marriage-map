# Births, Marriage & Boost Map

Interactive map of U.S. birth rates, married share, and the marriage "Boost" score, built from ACS 2020–2024 5-year estimates.

**View the map:** https://lymanstone.github.io/tract-births-marriage-map/

The map can show four levels: census tracts (the default), counties, metro areas, and states.

- **Birth rate and married share** at the county, metro, and state levels are the Census Bureau's own published estimates for each area.
- **Metro areas** are the 393 Metropolitan Statistical Areas (2023 definitions). Each state's counties outside them are grouped as one "Nonmetro" area.
- **Boost** at the county, metro, and state levels is the population-weighted average of the area's tract scores, i.e. the score the typical resident's tract earns. Each area's tooltip also shows the share of residents in tracts scoring 8 or 10, and the score the area would get if it were scored as one unit.
